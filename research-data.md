## 6. Gold Price Context

Gold hit an all-time high of US$5,589/oz on 28 January 2026, driven by US-Iran geopolitical tensions, record central bank buying (~60 tonnes/month vs pre-2022 average of 17 tonnes), and record ETF inflows ($89B in 2025). Gold gained 64% in 2025 — its best year since 1979.

The price subsequently corrected significantly, falling to ~US$4,091 by mid-March before recovering to ~US$4,541/oz by late March 2026 (A$6,611/oz at AUD/USD 0.687).

In AUD terms, Australian producers have benefited from both rising USD gold and a relatively weak AUD, with several producers reporting average realized prices of A$5,000–6,000/oz. This supercycle environment means all producers are generating record free cash flows, which has compressed Net Debt/EBITDA ratios across the sector (9 of 11 constituents now in net cash positions) and lifted market capitalizations significantly.
