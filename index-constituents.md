## 6. Constituent Profiles

### Evolution Mining (ASX: EVN) — Weight: 27.09%
Australia's premier mid-tier gold and copper producer operating five owned mines and one JV across New South Wales, Queensland, and Western Australia, plus Red Lake in Ontario, Canada (~16% of production). Sector-leading AISC of A$1,493/oz in H1 FY26. Record H1 underlying profit of A$785M (+104% YoY). Net debt reduced to A$362M (gearing 6%), on track for net cash by end-FY26. Ore Reserves of 11 Moz gold + 1.4 Mt copper support ~15-year mine life. Growth pipeline: Northparkes E22 block cave (A$545M capex, first ore FY30), Ernest Henry Bert deposit (A$160M capex). Quality multiplier of 1.128x — highest in the index — driven by lowest AISC (Score 5) and long reserve life (Score 5).

### Northern Star Resources (ASX: NST) — Weight: 26.71%
Australia's largest pure-play gold producer following the De Grey Mining (Hemi) acquisition. Member of the S&P/ASX 20. Operating three WA centres (Kalgoorlie/KCGM, Yandal, Pilbara/Hemi) and Pogo in Alaska (~17% of production). FY26 guidance of 1.7–1.85 Moz with a five-year pathway to 2 Moz/yr. KCGM mill expansion targeting 850–900 koz/yr by FY29. Largest reserve base in the index at an estimated ~20 Moz P&P. Quality multiplier of 1.038x reflects mid-range AISC (Score 2) offset by long reserve life (Score 5) and low leverage (Score 4).

### Ramelius Resources (ASX: RMS) — Weight: 8.35%
High-performing WA mid-tier producer using a "hub and spoke" model centred on Mt Magnet. Acquired Spartan Resources (Dalgaranga, 2.87 Moz resource) in 2025. Never Never PFS (Oct 2025) added 1.6 Moz maiden reserve, lifting group reserves to 4.2 Moz (up 118% YoY) — with Dalgaranga ore reserve declaration still pending. Net cash A$694M with A$250M buyback in progress. Added to FTSE All-World Index in March 2026. Pathway to 500+ koz/yr by FY30. Quality multiplier of 1.120x driven by strong AISC (Score 4) and exceptional reserve life of ~21.5 years (Score 5).

### Greatland Resources (ASX: GGP) — Weight: 7.25%
Rapidly transitioning from explorer to major producer via the Telfer acquisition (Dec 2024) and Havieron development. Gold represents ~91% of revenue with copper ~9%. Telfer mineral resource expanded 150% to 8.0 Moz in March 2026, bringing group total to 14.9 Moz. Havieron FS confirms a long-life, low-cost operation (266 koz/yr, AISC US$1,610/oz) with first gold targeted H2 2027. Cash A$948M, net cash ~A$920M. Quality multiplier of 1.120x — joint-highest among producers — driven by strong AISC (Score 4), long reserve life (Score 5), and net cash (Score 5).

### Capricorn Metals (ASX: CMM) — Weight: 7.03%
Single-mine producer at Karlawinda (WA) transitioning to a two-mine operator with Mt Gibson development. Lowest operating AISC among producers at A$1,627/oz. Exceptional reserve life of ~33 years, cash of A$441M, and zero debt contribute to the joint-highest Quality Score in the index (4.60) alongside RMS and GGP, and a 1.12x multiplier. Declared maiden interim dividend of A$0.05/share in February 2026.

### Genesis Minerals (ASX: GMD) — Weight: 6.46%
Emerging WA mid-tier producer operating across the Leonora and Laverton gold districts. Gwalia underground is a high-grade asset often exceeding 1 oz/t. FY26 guidance of 260–290 koz with the ASPIRE 500 strategy targeting 500+ koz/yr (upgraded from ASPIRE 400). Aggressive M&A (Laverton A$250M, Magnetic A$639M) has built a pro forma resource base of 21 Moz with 5.2 Moz reserves. Debt-free with A$404M cash. Strong reserve life (15.3 years) and net cash position offset by mid-range AISC.

### Westgold Resources (ASX: WGX) — Weight: 4.80%
Top-five Australian gold producer following the transformative Karora Resources merger. Operates across WA's Murchison and Southern Goldfields regions. 100% debt-free with A$521M cash. FY26 guidance of 345–385 koz. 3-year outlook targets 470 koz by FY28. Penalized on AISC (Score 1 — joint-highest in the universe) but rewarded for reserve life (~9.6 years, Score 4) and net cash (Score 5). Planning demerger of non-core projects into Valiant Gold Limited via IPO.

### Regis Resources (ASX: RRL) — Weight: 4.26%
Experienced WA gold producer operating Duketon (100% owned) and Tropicana (30% JV with AngloGold Ashanti). Now debt-free with A$930M cash + bullion — first time debt-free since entering production. FY26 guidance of 350–380 koz. Dividend tripled to A$0.15/share. Receives the lowest quality multiplier (0.993x) due to the shortest reserve life in the index (~4.7 years, Score 2) after McPhillamys reserves were withdrawn following the s10 ATSIHP Act ruling. McPhillamys judicial review decision expected Apr–May 2026; if eventually approved, would add ~200 koz/yr and 2.26 Moz reserves.

### Vault Minerals (ASX: VAU) — Weight: 4.03% — NEW
Formed from the June 2024 merger of Red 5 and Silver Lake Resources, renamed September 2024. Operates three WA gold mining centres: Leonora (King of the Hills + Darlot), Mount Monger, and Deflector. Sugar Zone (Ontario, Canada) is idled since August 2023 with restart targeted FY28 (~80 koz/yr); even post-restart, eligible jurisdictional production would remain ~80%. FY26 guidance of 332–360 koz at AISC A$2,650–2,850/oz. Cash A$537M, zero debt, nearly fully unhedged. Quality multiplier of 1.030x reflects high AISC (Score 1) offset by strong reserve life (~11.6 years, Score 5) and net cash (Score 5).

### Bellevue Gold (ASX: BGL) — Weight: 2.21%
Single high-grade underground mine in WA's Goldfields (9.0 g/t resource grade). Ramp-up has been challenging — FY25 guidance was cut twice, and a A$156.5M equity raise was required. FY26 targeting 130–150 koz with improvement into FY27 (175–195 koz). Balance sheet has improved to ~A$65M net cash (A$165M cash, A$100M debt). Quality multiplier of 1.008x reflects highest AISC in the index (Score 1) but improving balance sheet (now net cash, Score 5) and solid reserve life (9.6 years, Score 4). Exceptional grade and resource quality offer long-term upside. P&P reserves of 1.34 Moz.

### Rupert Resources (TSX: RUP) — Weight: 1.78%
The index's sole non-Australian constituent and only Finland-based company. Pre-production gold developer focused on the Ikkari discovery in Northern Finland's Central Lapland Greenstone Belt, adjacent to Agnico Eagle's Kittila mine. PFS outlines 3.5 Moz P&P reserves with 20-year mine life and industry-leading AISC of US$918/oz. First gold targeted 2030. DFS expected end of H1 2027 (Ausenco engaged); EIA submission targeted Q4 2026. Initial capex US$575M; NPV₅% of US$1.7B at US$2,150/oz gold. Scored under the Junior Developer Carve-Out (§5.2) with a default neutral Net Debt/EBITDA score of 3.0, resulting in a 1.105x quality multiplier. Market cap of C$1.66B (~A$1.7B). Agnico Eagle holds a 14.0% strategic stake (increased from 9.9% via multiple placements; board nominee Carol Plummer appointed).

## 8. Observations & Notes

1. **Geographic Concentration:** The quantitative jurisdictional filters continue to produce a portfolio heavily concentrated in Western Australia, now with 11 constituents (up from 10 following Vault Minerals' inclusion). The sole non-Australian constituent is Rupert Resources (Finland) at 1.78%. No NZ company currently meets the combined Base Universe + exposure thresholds. OceanaGold fails due to Haile (USA) and Didipio (Philippines) exposure. Agnico Eagle fails due to Canadian production dominance.

2. **No Hard-Cap Binding:** With 11 constituents, EVN's normalized weight of 27.09% falls well below the 30% single-stock cap. The addition of Vault Minerals has diluted the top-two concentration versus the 10-stock portfolio. No capping or redistribution is required.

3. **Quality Tilt Impact:** The quality tilt remains modest by design (±15%). The biggest beneficiary is EVN, which now achieves the highest Composite Score (4.70) and a 1.128x multiplier — overtaking NST by weight despite having a smaller FFMC (A$25.2B vs A$27.0B). This is driven by EVN's superior AISC (Score 5) and corrected reserve life (~14.8 years, Score 5). The most penalized is RRL, receiving a 0.993x multiplier due to its short reserve life (~4.7 years, Score 2) — the only constituent with a multiplier below 1.0x.

4. **Junior Developer Carve-Out Applied:** Rupert Resources (RUP) is scored under §5.2, using PFS life-of-mine AISC and reserve life estimates, with a default neutral Net Debt/EBITDA score of 3.0. The DFS is expected end of H1 2027; once available, PFS-based scores will be replaced with DFS figures.

5. **Vault Minerals Added:** The Silver Lake / Red 5 merged entity (renamed Vault Minerals, ASX: VAU, effective 30 Sep 2024) passes all eligibility criteria: market cap A$4.1B (well above USD $350M), 100% WA production currently, >90% gold revenue, 4.0 Moz P&P reserves, and zero debt. Its quality profile (Composite 3.40, multiplier 1.030x) is similar to Westgold — high AISC dragged by the Mt Monger and Deflector cost profiles, but long reserve life and net cash provide offsets.
